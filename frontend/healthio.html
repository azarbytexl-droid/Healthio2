Home first aid while help is coming

These are basic safety steps, not home treatment. Call emergency services first for severe or worsening symptoms; do not wait for a doctor to arrive through this app.

  • Stay with the person, keep them as calm and comfortable as possible, and follow the emergency operator's instructions.
  • Unlock the entrance and ask someone to guide responders to the patient. Share the exact address, symptoms, and when they began.
  • If the person is unconscious but breathing normally, place them on their side if there is no suspected neck or back injury. Do not give food, drink, or medicine.
  • If they are not breathing normally, call emergency services on speakerphone and start CPR only if you know how or the operator guides you.
  • Use an emergency medicine only if it was prescribed for that person, and follow its label or their clinician's instructions.
Doctor home visits are not available through HEALTHIO. This app cannot dispatch a doctor or promise an immediate home check. Contact your local clinic directly for a home visit; if the condition may be life-threatening, call emergency services now.

Minor cut

Apply steady pressure with clean cloth or gauze. Once bleeding is controlled, rinse with clean running water and cover. Seek urgent care for heavy or uncontrolled bleeding or a deep wound.

Burn

Cool a small thermal burn under cool running water for 20 minutes. Do not apply ice, butter, or creams. Seek urgent care for large, deep, chemical, electrical, face, or airway burns.

Possible stroke

Note when symptoms started and call emergency services immediately for face drooping, arm weakness, or speech difficulty. Do not give food, drink, or medication.

Basic first-aid education only. It is not a diagnosis or a replacement for emergency care. Guidance: American Red Cross first-aid steps.

Food and hydration

Aim for varied, balanced meals and safe food handling. Wash produce; choose thoroughly cooked foods; avoid alcohol. Food guidance differs by country and personal health, so ask your prenatal clinician about your diet and any restrictions.

NHS foods to avoid in pregnancy.

Movement and exercise

If your clinician says exercise is suitable for you, gentle activities such as walking may be options. ACOG describes 150 minutes of moderate activity weekly for many uncomplicated pregnancies; this is not a personal target. Ask your clinician before starting or changing exercise.
